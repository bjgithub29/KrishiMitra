# Banana (Kela) — Crop & Task Guide (All-India)

**Scientific name:** Musa spp.  
**Reference variety:** Grand Naine

## Major Growing Regions in India

- **Maharashtra** (Jalgaon, Solapur) — commonly grown variety: Grand Naine

- **Tamil Nadu** (Trichy, Theni) — commonly grown variety: Poovan, Grand Naine

- **Gujarat** (Surat, Bharuch) — commonly grown variety: Grand Naine

- **Andhra Pradesh** (East Godavari) — commonly grown variety: Grand Naine

## Growing Conditions (National Standard)

- **Season:** Annual, sown in June (varies +/- 2-4 weeks by latitude/region)

- **Duration:** 330 days from sowing to harvest

- **Soil pH:** ideal 6.0–7.5, tolerated 5.5–8.0

- **Soil EC limit:** up to 2.0 dS/m

- **Soil texture:** loam, clay loam; drainage: well-drained

- **Rainfall requirement:** 1200–2000 mm

- **Temperature range:** 15–35°C

- **Irrigation type:** drip

- **Drought tolerance:** Low | **Salinity tolerance:** Low | **Waterlogging tolerance:** Low

## Nutrient Requirement (per hectare)

- Target yield: 600.0 quintal/ha

- N: 200 kg | P: 60 kg | K: 300 kg

- Zinc need: High | Iron need: High

## Fertilizer Plan (Actual Products & Doses)

**Basal (at sowing / land prep)**  
Products: DAP: 130.4 kg/ha, MOP: 500.0 kg/ha, Urea (top-up): 166.4 kg/ha, + FYM 5-10 t/ha during land prep  
Purpose: Supplies full P, full K, half of N; FYM improves soil structure and micronutrient availability

**Top-dress 1 (~30-40% crop duration, vegetative)**  
Products: Urea: 108.7 kg/ha  
Purpose: Boosts vegetative growth; apply after weeding, with soil moisture present

**Top-dress 2 (~55-65% crop duration, flowering/reproductive)**  
Products: Urea: 108.7 kg/ha, Zinc Sulphate: 20 kg/ha (if High Zn deficiency indicated by soil test)  
Purpose: Supports flowering/grain-fill; most yield-critical nitrogen application

## Common Pests & Diseases

- **Pests:** banana weevil, aphid

- **Diseases:** panama wilt, sigatoka leaf spot

## Stage-wise Task Calendar

*Day ranges are counted from sowing date; adjust actual calendar dates based on the region's sowing window above.*

### Land Preparation (Day -30 to 0 from sowing)

- **[Land Prep] Pit/field preparation** (One-time, priority: Medium): Dig pits or prepare beds with FYM incorporation.

- **[Land Prep] Layout & spacing** (One-time, priority: Medium): Mark planting layout per recommended spacing.

### Planting/Establishment (Day 0 to 30 from sowing)

- **[Sowing] Transplanting/Planting** (One-time, priority: Medium): Plant healthy seedlings/suckers/setts at correct depth.

- **[Irrigation] Establishment irrigation** (Every 3-5 days, priority: High): Irrigate immediately after planting and at frequent intervals.

- **[Monitoring] Gap filling** (One-time, priority: Medium): Replace dead/weak plants within first month.

### Vegetative Growth (Day 30 to 150 from sowing)

- **[Fertilizer] Basal + split fertilizer** (Monthly, priority: High): Apply NPK in split doses as per crop stage.

- **[Irrigation] Regular irrigation** (Weekly, priority: High): Maintain consistent moisture as per crop water requirement.

- **[Weeding] Weed control** (Monthly, priority: Medium): Remove weeds/mulch to conserve moisture and reduce competition.

- **[Pest Scouting] Routine pest scouting** (Weekly, priority: High): Check for borers, mites, and sucking pests.

### Flowering/Fruiting (Day 150 to 250 from sowing)

- **[Fertilizer] Flowering-stage nutrition** (Monthly, priority: High): Apply K-rich fertilizer to support flowering/fruit set.

- **[Irrigation] Critical stage irrigation** (Weekly, priority: High): Avoid moisture stress during flowering/fruit development.

- **[Disease Scouting] Fungal/bacterial disease check** (Weekly, priority: Medium): Inspect for wilt/leaf spot/anthracnose, especially in humid weather.

### Maturity & Harvest (Day 250 to 330 from sowing)

- **[Monitoring] Maturity indicators** (Weekly, priority: Medium): Check color/size/firmness for harvest readiness.

- **[Harvest] Harvesting** (As needed, priority: Medium): Harvest in stages as produce matures.

- **[Post-Harvest] Grading & storage** (One-time, priority: Medium): Grade, pack and store/transport produce promptly.
