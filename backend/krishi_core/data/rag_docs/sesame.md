# Sesame (Til) — Crop & Task Guide (All-India)

**Scientific name:** Sesamum indicum  
**Reference variety:** GT-2

## Major Growing Regions in India

- **Rajasthan** (Jaipur, Alwar) — commonly grown variety: RT-346

- **Madhya Pradesh** (Guna, Vidisha) — commonly grown variety: JT-7

- **Gujarat** (Banaskantha, Mehsana) — commonly grown variety: GT-2

- **West Bengal** (Murshidabad) — commonly grown variety: local

## Growing Conditions (National Standard)

- **Season:** Kharif, sown in July (varies +/- 2-4 weeks by latitude/region)

- **Duration:** 90 days from sowing to harvest

- **Soil pH:** ideal 5.5–8.0, tolerated 5.0–8.5

- **Soil EC limit:** up to 1.5 dS/m

- **Soil texture:** sandy loam, loam; drainage: well-drained

- **Rainfall requirement:** 400–650 mm

- **Temperature range:** 25–38°C

- **Irrigation type:** rainfed

- **Drought tolerance:** High | **Salinity tolerance:** Low | **Waterlogging tolerance:** Low

## Nutrient Requirement (per hectare)

- Target yield: 6.0 quintal/ha

- N: 40 kg | P: 20 kg | K: 20 kg

- Zinc need: Low | Iron need: Low

## Fertilizer Plan (Actual Products & Doses)

**Basal (at sowing / land prep)**  
Products: DAP: 43.5 kg/ha, MOP: 33.3 kg/ha, Urea (top-up): 26.5 kg/ha, + FYM 5-10 t/ha during land prep  
Purpose: Supplies full P, full K, half of N; FYM improves soil structure and micronutrient availability

**Top-dress 1 (~30-40% crop duration, vegetative)**  
Products: Urea: 21.7 kg/ha  
Purpose: Boosts vegetative growth; apply after weeding, with soil moisture present

**Top-dress 2 (~55-65% crop duration, flowering/reproductive)**  
Products: Urea: 21.7 kg/ha  
Purpose: Supports flowering/grain-fill; most yield-critical nitrogen application

## Common Pests & Diseases

- **Pests:** gall fly, leaf webber

- **Diseases:** phyllody, leaf spot

## Stage-wise Task Calendar

*Day ranges are counted from sowing date; adjust actual calendar dates based on the region's sowing window above.*

### Land Preparation (Day -14 to 0 from sowing)

- **[Land Prep] Deep ploughing** (One-time, priority: Medium): Plough field 20-25cm deep to break hardpan and expose pests to sun.

- **[Land Prep] Apply FYM/compost** (One-time, priority: Medium): Incorporate well-rotted farmyard manure 8-10 t/ha before sowing.

- **[Land Prep] Leveling & bunding** (One-time, priority: Medium): Level field and form bunds/ridges as per irrigation method.

### Sowing (Day 0 to 3 from sowing)

- **[Sowing] Seed treatment** (One-time, priority: Medium): Treat seed with recommended fungicide/rhizobium culture before sowing.

- **[Sowing] Sowing operation** (One-time, priority: Medium): Sow at recommended spacing and depth for the variety.

- **[Irrigation] Pre-sowing irrigation** (One-time, priority: High): Ensure adequate soil moisture at sowing; irrigate if dry.

### Germination & Establishment (Day 3 to 14 from sowing)

- **[Monitoring] Germination count** (Weekly, priority: Medium): Check germination percentage 7-10 days after sowing; gap-fill if needed.

- **[Irrigation] Light irrigation** (As needed, priority: High): Apply light irrigation to aid establishment, avoid waterlogging.

- **[Weeding] First hand weeding** (One-time, priority: Medium): Remove weeds competing with young seedlings.

### Vegetative Growth (Day 14 to 40 from sowing)

- **[Fertilizer] First top dressing** (One-time, priority: High): Apply first split dose of Nitrogen as per soil test.

- **[Irrigation] Scheduled irrigation** (Every 7-10 days, priority: High): Irrigate at critical vegetative stage based on soil moisture/ET.

- **[Weeding] Inter-culturing** (Bi-weekly, priority: Medium): Hoe/inter-cultivate to control weeds and aerate soil.

- **[Pest Scouting] Scout for sucking pests** (Weekly, priority: High): Monitor for aphid/whitefly/jassid; apply need-based control.

### Flowering / Reproductive (Day 40 to 63 from sowing)

- **[Fertilizer] Second top dressing** (One-time, priority: High): Apply remaining N and K dose at flowering/pre-flowering stage.

- **[Irrigation] Critical stage irrigation** (Every 5-7 days, priority: High): Ensure no moisture stress during flowering - most yield-sensitive stage.

- **[Pest Scouting] Monitor pod/fruit/boll borers** (Weekly, priority: High): Install pheromone traps; scout and spray if ETL crossed.

- **[Disease Scouting] Fungal disease check** (Weekly, priority: Medium): Inspect for blight/rust/mildew symptoms after rain or high humidity.

### Maturity & Pre-Harvest (Day 63 to 86 from sowing)

- **[Irrigation] Reduce/stop irrigation** (One-time, priority: High): Withhold irrigation 10-15 days before harvest to aid maturity/drying.

- **[Monitoring] Maturity check** (Weekly, priority: Medium): Check grain/pod moisture and visual maturity indicators.

- **[Pest Scouting] Final pest check** (One-time, priority: High): Check for storage pests before harvest; avoid late-season sprays near harvest.

### Harvest & Post-Harvest (Day 86 to 90 from sowing)

- **[Harvest] Harvesting** (One-time, priority: Medium): Harvest at physiological maturity to minimize shattering/quality loss.

- **[Post-Harvest] Drying & cleaning** (One-time, priority: Medium): Dry produce to safe moisture level and clean before storage/sale.

- **[Post-Harvest] Storage** (One-time, priority: Medium): Store in proper conditions to prevent pest/fungal damage.
